NEW IN 0.5.0

Your documents, side by side.

Open PDF, Word DOCX, TXT, or Markdown in the document reader. PDF keeps its original page on the left, with translated paragraphs on the right. Word and text use aligned reading paragraphs. Translate one page or the full document, synchronize scrolling, stop and resume, and export bilingual TXT.

For a PDF already open in Edge, choose PDF / 文档左右对照 in the extension popup and open the prefilled link. Or choose a local file. Only an explicit translation action sends extracted text to your configured API. Files are parsed locally; original file bytes are not uploaded to the translation provider.

Maximum 25 MB, 300 pages/reading sections, and one million extracted characters. Scanned PDFs need external OCR first. Word images, table layout, headers, footers, and original pagination are not reproduced. Complex PDF reading order may need checking. Requires Chromium-based Edge 125+.
